# Provenance and attribution

goodWriter contains original writing instructions and synthesized guidance. It was developed with AI assistance from private classroom-report examples, reflective-writing resources, and public writing skills.

The private classroom material is not a public dataset and is not redistributed. Its use informed the design; it does not provide reproducible evidence that the skill improves grades. No original report, student name or ID, source code submission, or teacher-feedback screenshot is included in this repository.

Public influences and the parts adopted or rejected are listed in [sources.md](good-writer/references/sources.md). They include the Universities of Edinburgh, Waterloo, UNSW and Monash, and the `content-research-writer`, `writing-clearly-and-concisely`, and `humanizer` skills. The project does not bundle upstream skill implementations. Original source licenses continue to apply to their respective materials.

Examples and pilot inputs published here are synthetic. Pilot outputs are model-generated and labeled by the method that produced them. They are examples of observed behavior, not student work or endorsements by the compared projects.

The public alpha changes the private development default: users receive final prose without an unsolicited self-score. The diagnostic rubric remains available for requested reviews. The previously reported internal design score is not a public effectiveness claim.
